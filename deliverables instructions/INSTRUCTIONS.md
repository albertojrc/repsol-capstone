## Deliverables

1. **Written report** — min 10 pages, max 20 pages, 1.0 line spacing, 12-point Times New Roman.
2. **Poster** — PDF, A0 format.
3. **Final presentation** — slides prepared for a 15-minute presentation.
4. **Code** — GitHub repository, accessible notebook.
5. **Ethics statement** — on the cover page.

## Required Report Structure

- **Objective** — define the problem and the aim of the work.
- **Data Sources** — detail the data used, including preparation/processing and acquisition of any additional data.
- **Methodology** — describe the tools, processes, and technology employed in problem-solving.
- **Potential Solutions** — present **at least two** considered alternatives, explaining the rationale for the chosen solution.
- **Development** — include the code, following standard practices for clarity and understanding by field specialists.
- **Conclusions** — summarize the final result from a technical standpoint (algorithm choice, precision/metrics) and discuss its impact and practicality for business implementation.

## Poster Presentation Goals

- Clear communication: summarize key insights quickly and effectively.
- Visual storytelling: showcase data and solutions through strong visuals.
- Real-world simulation: practice business-style, executive-level presentation.
- Interactive engagement: discuss, explain, and defend the work dynamically.
- Portfolio material: a professional artifact for interviews/networking.

## Course Objectives (what's being assessed)

- Identify and analyze the business challenge, then propose a data-driven solution.
- Choose the most suitable data science / ML / AI model and methodology, weighing trade-offs among alternatives.
- Create a sustainable architecture that supports the selected model.
- Develop a proof of concept using real-world data and the tools/techniques learned in the program.
- Present the proposal and findings to a panel with clarity and precision.
- Acknowledge and address any ethical or legal implications of the proposed solution.

## Evaluation Criteria

- **Individual academic tutor feedback: 40%**
- **Project report and presentation: 60%** (graded by 2 academics/professors + the company mentor; half of this 60% is individual, half is group)
  - Originality of the work and relevance of the proposed contribution: 25%
  - Methodological rigor and justification of the decisions made: 25%
  - Coherence, quality, and clarity in the written, oral, and visual presentation: 25%
  - Critical analysis and ability to defend the results in front of the evaluation committee: 25%
